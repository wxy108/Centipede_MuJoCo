# Separately declared time-varying contact erasure

Prepared before any contact dynamics. This channel supplements persistent loss and must remain separately labeled. Amplitudes are exactly the same independently optimized, qualified and frozen no-loss bank. Fixed PD, body/leg wave, frequency, model, friction and physics remain unchanged.

At t=1.5 s and every0.5 s thereafter, independently command each of19bilateral pairs to lose ground collision with Bernoulli probability p. The same uniform matrix is used across waves and probability levels; longer trials share the shorter schedule prefix. p is the desired Bernoulli probability, not the measured erased-time fraction. Internal robot collisions and all chassis-ground collisions remain intact.

Disable both legs immediately when a pair is commanded lost. When its command returns to available, restore each leg independently only after all its actual colliders clear the ground plane by at least50micrometres. Check current-pose kinematics at1ms intervals. Bilateral legs are allowed to recover separately because alternating gait may never place both in swing simultaneously. Exact geometric plane support heights include capsule, sphere, box, cylinder, ellipsoid and compiled mesh colliders; validate against MuJoCo's actual plane-contact distances before use. Never restore a geometrically penetrating leg. Record every restoration's clearance and every delayed erased-time fraction.

This rule can make actual exposure wave-dependent even with identical desired random draws. Always plot/report actual exposure and delay alongside p. Do not claim identical actual exposure or pure redundancy evidence if a crossing is explained by recovery delays. Requested masks are nested in p; the resulting dynamic restoration paths need not be nested.

Before any transient erasure dynamics, finish amplitude qualification and freeze selection. p=0 must reproduce the nominal displacement and all original metrics exactly. Use all qualified waves and p=[0,.2,.4,.6,.8,1], discoveryseeds0..3; endpoints deterministic. Preserve all failures and incomplete outcomes. Any chosen crossing requires a separate frozen pair/bracket, new pairedseeds100..119, and half-timestep/long-duration checks. Never pool this channel with persistent results or reuse confirmation masks for pair selection.

Pair selection uses the same predeclared order as the persistent analysis: first prefer both waves upright with mean chassis-contact fraction<=.15 and positive mean displacement at both bracket endpoints. Within that category prefer pairs whose frozen low-wave body amplitude is greater than the high-wave body amplitude, then the largest two-sided discovery sign margin. The high wave must remain upright in at least75% of discovery masks and retain meanD>=max(5mm,10%of its baseline). If no clean pair qualifies, apply the amplitude/margin order to useful-high-wave crossings, explicitly labeling low-wave failures. Preserve every candidate pair. A restored-contact exposure difference is reported as a possible mechanism, never hidden.
