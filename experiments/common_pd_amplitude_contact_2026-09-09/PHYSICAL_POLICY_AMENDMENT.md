# Physical-geometry branch, declared 2026-09-09 before its search

The original September 3 policy remains the comparison branch. The new `physical_geometry_v2` branch replaces only its `spine_tortuosity_max <= 1.2` gate with absence of actual penetrating contact between nonadjacent chassis collision geometries, observed at every physics step from 1.5 s. The old tortuosity value remains a diagnostic. All other engineering gates, amplitude bounds, phase convention, fixed common PD, plant, solver step, objective, and initialization are identical. No trend or crossover constraint is added.

Reason: the exact current geometry imposes ideal joint-amplitude caps 7.76, 15.57, 23.34 and 30.66 degrees at waves 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 under R<=1.2; waves >=2.5 can use the full 36 degree domain. R measures distributed curvature as well as folding. The transferred RL wave 1.5 gait fails only R, with a regular centerline and no nonadjacent chassis contacts in 270 saved poses. That is evidence that R is an unsuitable universal collision/folding proxy; it is not proof that the new optimum will match the desired trend. Continuous collision observation is required in the new branch, because the saved poses alone cannot establish continuous safety. Mesh-distance calls returned ambiguous zero values and are not used as clearance proof. Mesh-box overlaps likewise do not establish collisions.

Physical trajectories of complete old-branch trials may be reused for search proposals because changing an observer/acceptance rule does not change dynamics. Such imported records explicitly have unverified continuous self-contact and are provisional. They cannot be final selections. Old prefix records can only be reused when they prove violation of a still-active gate; a spine-only prefix must be rerun. All finalists get fresh full 6 s, 12 s and half-step runs with continuous self-contact observation. An exact trajectory and original-metric parity check precedes search use.

This is a disclosed policy correction, not a change to old data or to the frozen original protocol. Both curves and exclusions must be reported. All output is isolated under `outputs/physical_geometry_v2`; Clean and historical experiments remain untouched.
