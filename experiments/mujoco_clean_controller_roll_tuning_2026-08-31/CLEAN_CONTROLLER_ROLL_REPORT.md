# Centipede Model clean-controller roll tuning

Date: 2026-08-31  
Scope: abstract/biological Centipede Model only. Centipede Robot Step 18/19 is out of scope.

## Outcome

The projected-gravity feedback controller was rolled back and marked rejected. The frozen locked-head controller and MJCF files were not edited. The selected replacement is a terrain-profile parameter for the original open-loop CPG:

- flat: multiply the existing leg-pitch amplitude by `0.525`;
- rough: keep the existing leg-pitch amplitude at `1.0`;
- keep body/leg wave numbers coupled (`1.5/1.5` flat, `3/3` rough);
- keep relative leg phase at `0 deg`;
- add no projected-gravity, contact, or state feedback;
- add no active body-roll torque;
- change no mass, inertia, armature, geometry, topology, passive stiffness, or damping.

This is a load-time terrain parameter table, not an online terrain detector or a state-dependent rule. The controller equation remains the original `LeanController.step()` path.

## Six-second validation

| Terrain | Configuration | Forward displacement | Coherent roll P95 | Front-7 roll P95 | Mean contacting feet | Tip-roll P95 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| flat | locked baseline | 143.74 mm | 16.34 deg | 18.70 deg | 13.90 / 38 | 27.43 deg |
| flat | selected | 133.04 mm | 5.13 deg | 7.51 deg | 18.41 / 38 | 29.91 deg |
| rough | locked baseline | 91.14 mm | 8.68 deg | 13.38 deg | 13.21 / 38 | 26.73 deg |
| rough | selected | 91.14 mm | 8.68 deg | 13.38 deg | 13.21 / 38 | 26.73 deg |

Flat coherent roll falls by `68.6%`, front-7 roll falls by `59.8%`, and forward displacement retention is `92.6%`. Rough is bit-for-bit numerically unchanged in the recomputed rows because its applied scale is `1.0`.

The flat trade-off is explicit: the target leg-pitch amplitude changes from about `36.9 deg` to `19.4 deg`, and mean contacting feet rises by about `4.5`. The side-view temporal montage still shows a moving swing wave rather than a frozen or fully dragging leg set. The passive short-tip roll remains active (`29.91 deg` P95) and is not locked to obtain stability.

All final rows are finite and warning-free; armature remains zero everywhere; total mass remains `2.536031 g`; no active roll torque is produced; topology and frozen-model hashes are unchanged.

## Parameter families rejected

| Family | Evidence-based reason for rejection |
|---|---|
| projected-gravity feedback | Rejected by the PI because it polluted the clean controller architecture. Historical outputs are retained only as noncanonical evidence. |
| zero-target active body-roll PD | Reduced local twist but increased coherent/global roll as gain rose; high gains also exceeded the roll-torque gate. |
| leg-pitch stiffness or damping alone | Damping had negligible effect; sufficiently low stiffness damaged propulsion and support before roll was removed. |
| body-wave amplitude | Lower body amplitude reduced forward displacement without reducing coherent roll. |
| leg-yaw amplitude | Smaller yaw lost displacement; larger yaw restored displacement but did not remove front roll and drove tip roll toward its limit. |
| passive body-roll k1-k6 | Lower K redistributed common roll into excessive local twist; rough front roll worsened. Higher K reproduced the rigid common-mode tilt. |
| passive tip stiffness/damping | More damping had negligible effect; softer tips worsened roll; 1.5x stiffness suppressed intended compliance; 2x stiffness caused failure. |
| leg-pitch amplitude below 0.50 | Produced more continuous contact and unacceptable displacement loss before rough roll met the flat-ground target. |

## Demo and reproducibility

The final demo contains front, side, and top views for both flat and rough terrain. Every video has `121` frames at `30 fps` (`4.03 s`). The demo is generated by the same candidate used for validation; there is no presentation-only parameter set.

Run the final validation:

```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH=(Resolve-Path '.python_packages')
python experiments\mujoco_clean_controller_roll_tuning_2026-08-31\run_clean_roll_tuning.py screen --duration 6 --kinds flat rough --candidates baseline terrain_profile_lp0525
```

Render the demo:

```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH=(Resolve-Path '.python_packages')
```
