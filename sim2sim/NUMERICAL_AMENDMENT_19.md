# Nonlinear correction convergence is required before full-gait use

The contact-law correction passes isolated tests, including continuous physical X PD without per-step state resetting. However, the active-ramp full-robot stress test does not converge in 20 fixed-point updates. Cold initialization fails 609/1000 steps (max residual 0.000394 m/s), warm initialization 408/1000 (0.000118 m/s); their generalized positions differ by 0.0121. Therefore the two unfinished cold 20-update whole-gait workers were stopped by verified owned process ID; their partial trajectories and interruption receipts are retained, and no complete gait result is claimed.

Test Anderson acceleration of the same De Saxce fixed-point equation in velocity units, with history length six, safeguard against large residual growth, nonnegative correction and unchanged residual tolerance 1e-8 m/s. Compare 60 versus 120 iteration budgets on the same 1000-step active-ramp stress test before any new full gait. This changes only root finding, not mu, PD, geometry, the contact equation, scores or acceptance criteria. The isolated success remains valid evidence of a contact-model difference; it is not yet an adopted full-robot solver.
